What it means

Easier They send to a competitor

Their patients already go to Soleo, Option Care, Horizon or Infusion Express. Pitch: local nurse, faster start, someone answers the phone. Ask for the next new start.

Medium Part of their patients go out

They infuse some in-house but send a share out (often home IG). Go after that share, not the whole practice.

Hard / Very hard They infuse themselves

Own infusion suite or a health system. Don't pitch replacing it. Offer overflow and home infusion only, and don't route weekly.
